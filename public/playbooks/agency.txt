FOREIGN COLORS | Agency PLAYBOOK
Research-informed application, not a proven outcome.

TITLE: Find the reason before writing the slogan.

IDEA: Build an audience brief around first contact, understanding, preference and identification. Separate people who enjoy the sport from those who identify with the client’s club.

BASIS: The PCM separates levels of connection; identity research adds depth.

OBJECTIVE: Give a club a defensible account of whom it is trying to reach and what is unknown.

KPI: Documented audience segments, evidence behind each and unresolved research questions.

TEST: Interview a varied sample, compare contradictory accounts and validate proposed segments in a later sample.

CAUTION: A convenient sample of committed supporters does not represent all Americans. Do not promise causal conclusions from interviews.
